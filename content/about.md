I’m **N. Hrushikesh Rao**, a software engineer based in Hyderabad, India. I work on backend systems, with a particular interest in performance, reliability, and the details that make software work well in production.

Today, I work at **Goldman Sachs** on post-trade settlement for OTC derivatives. The work has me thinking about how distributed payment flows behave under real operational demands, and how to make critical systems dependable.
